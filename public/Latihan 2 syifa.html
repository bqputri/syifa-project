🍰 Pancake Recipe

🍽 Serves: 2–3 ⏱ 15 Minutes 💗 Sweet & Cozy

🧁 Ingredients

  • 120g flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 50g sugar
  • Milk as needed
  • Baking powder
  • Favorite toppings

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Mix flour, sugar & baking powder.
  2. Whisk eggs & milk.
  3. Combine until smooth.
  4. Pour into heated pan.
  5. Flip when bubbles appear.
  6. Add toppings & serve ✨

💡 Sweet Tips

  • Add vanilla for extra aroma.
  • Cook on low heat for fluffy texture.
  • Top with strawberries & honey 🍯
